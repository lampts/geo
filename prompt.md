# Prompt collection — Vietnamese banking (customer-centric)

30 prompts, grouped by the Home Credit customer-centricity pillars
(Motivator → Differentiator → Hygienic → Low priority). Each asks from the
customer's lived need and is asked on all answer models. `attribute` = the exact
brand attribute from the framework; `product` = banking product/service context.

Source of truth: `data/prompts.json`. Pillar distribution: Motivator 10 ·
Differentiator 11 · Hygienic 6 · Low priority 3.
