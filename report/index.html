Representative quotes from the AI answers — the trail behind the numbers.

Methodology: 30 prompts about Vietnamese banking products/services, each asked on every available model. Answers parsed for brand mentions (alias matching), citations (URL extraction) and per-brand sentiment (LLM-scored). Metrics aggregated into visibility %, share of voice, sentiment and source mix; insights written by Opus 4.8. Sentiment is suppressed for any brand with fewer than 10 mentions (too few to score reliably). Each brand domain is also probed for an llms.txt file (llmstxt.org) as an AI-readiness signal.
